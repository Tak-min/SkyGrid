Common questions

What is included for free?
Daily captures, buddies, alarms, and your newest 30 days of sky photos. The archive window rolls forward each day. Pro unlocks your full photo archive and full-year share card.

I missed my morning photo — is my streak gone?
Sky Grid gives every account one Rest Day per week, so a single missed morning doesn't necessarily break your streak. Check the Sky Grid tab to see which days counted.

How do I remove a buddy or report a photo?
Open the buddy's profile from the Today or Sky Grid screen — you'll find Report and Remove Buddy there. Reports are reviewed by us and are never visible to other users.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, streak history, and buddy relationships, and cannot be undone.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings (iOS) → your name → Subscriptions, and select Sky Grid.
