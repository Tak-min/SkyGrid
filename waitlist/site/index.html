How it works

Three seconds a day, one grid a year.

  1. 01

    One sky a day

    Open the app once each morning, point it at the sky, and let it go. Sky Grid reads the color and puts it away for you — no filters, no decisions, no scrolling.

  2. 02

    A year, one grid

    Every morning becomes a single tile. Three hundred and sixty-five of them make a grid that's really your own — and any stretch of it turns into a share card in one tap.

  3. 03

    Do it with someone

    Invite one trusted person. Your grids stay softly blurred to each other until you've both shown up for the morning — a small, quiet way to keep a promise together.

What do I do each morning?

Every morning becomes a single tile. Three hundred and sixty-five of them make a grid that's really your own — a piece of pixel art only you could have made.

Sky Grid is free to download and use. A Pro upgrade is available if you want it, but it's never required to keep your streak going.
