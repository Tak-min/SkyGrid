Got questions?

FAQ

What do I do each morning?

Take one sky photo a day. It joins your growing mosaic, and Moku is there to celebrate.

What happens to a year of mornings?

Your photos build a mosaic of your mornings. Open a day to revisit its sky photo: the newest 30 days are available free, and Pro unlocks your full photo archive.

Can I do this with a friend?

Invite your friends. Your morning skies unlock for each other once you have both posted that day.

Does it cost anything?

Daily captures, buddies, alarms, and your newest 30 days of sky photos are free. That archive window rolls forward each day. Pro unlocks your full photo archive and full-year share card, with monthly, annual, or one-time lifetime options. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Where can I get it?

Sky Grid is live on the App Store now — search "Sky Grid" or use the download button above.
